Services

What can we help you with?

Every service available at the centre, in plain language. Filter by the kind of care you need.

  • Medical male circumcision

    A safe, confidential procedure that lowers the risk of HIV and some STIs. Free of charge.

    Dr S.P. Lerutla

  • Dialysis

    Cleaning the blood of waste and extra fluid when the kidneys can no longer do it on their own.

    Lebatle Renal Care

  • Peritoneal dialysis

    Dialysis that uses the natural lining of the belly as a filter — often done at home.

    Lebatle Renal Care

  • Continuous renal replacement therapy

    Slow, continuous dialysis for patients who are very ill and need gentle, round-the-clock support.

    Lebatle Renal Care

  • Plasma exchange

    Removing and replacing the liquid part of the blood to treat certain kidney and immune conditions.

    Lebatle Renal Care

  • Kidney wellness clinic

    Check-ups and advice to protect your kidneys — important if you live with diabetes or high blood pressure.

    Lebatle Renal Care

  • Hearing tests

    Hearing checks for adults and children, with ongoing follow-up and maintenance.

    T Mailola

  • Hearing aids

    Fitting your hearing aid, showing you how to use it, and keeping it working well.

    T Mailola

  • Ear wax removal

    Safe removal of blocked ear wax, plus treatment and management of ear infections.

    T Mailola

  • Neonatal hearing screening

    Early hearing checks for newborn babies, so any problem is picked up as soon as possible.

    T Mailola

  • Speech therapy

    Support with speech, language and communication for both adults and children.

    T Mailola

  • Dysphagia care

    Help for babies, children and adults who have difficulty feeding or swallowing.

    T Mailola
